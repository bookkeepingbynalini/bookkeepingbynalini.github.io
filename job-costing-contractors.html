Job Costing Help for Contractors

See which jobs actually make money. I help contractors set up and maintain job costing in QuickBooks Online — so profit is tracked project by project, not guessed at year-end.

Most contractors know their total revenue but couldn't tell you which jobs made money and which ones lost it. That's the gap job costing closes. Done right, it shows the true profit on every project — materials, labor, and costs tracked job by job — so you can bid smarter and stop guessing.

How I help with job costing

  • Setup done right — I help configure job costing in QuickBooks Online for your business, with a structure that matches how you actually run jobs.
  • Ongoing maintenance — as part of monthly bookkeeping, I keep costs assigned to the right jobs so your project profit stays accurate.
  • Reports you can use — see profit by job, so you know which kinds of work to chase and which to price differently.
  • A foundation for better bids — real numbers from past jobs make your next estimate sharper.

Why it matters for contractors

Two jobs can look identical on the surface and end up wildly different on the bottom line. Without job costing, that difference is invisible until it's too late. With it, you can see patterns — which crews, which job types, which clients are actually profitable — and make decisions based on facts instead of gut feel.
